Privacy Policy

Your Privacy

Your privacy is respected when visiting The Weight of the Pulpit website. This page explains, in general terms, how information may be handled while you access this website.

The Weight of the Pulpit website does not require visitors to create an account in order to read the content currently published on the site.

The website does not intentionally collect personal information directly from visitors unless that information is voluntarily provided through a future contact method or service connected to the website.

Website Hosting

This website may be hosted through a third party hosting provider. Hosting providers and related technical services may automatically process limited technical information necessary to deliver the website, maintain security, or operate their services.

Fonts and External Resources

This website may use third party resources, including online font services. When your browser loads those resources, the provider may receive technical information necessary to deliver the requested content.

Cookies and Tracking

The Weight of the Pulpit website does not currently operate its own advertising or visitor tracking system for the purpose of building personal profiles.

Third party hosting providers, browsers, or external services may use technologies such as cookies or technical logs according to their own policies and the services being used.

External Links

If this website includes links to other websites or services, those external websites operate independently and may have their own privacy policies. Visitors are encouraged to review the policies of external services before providing personal information.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended primarily as a reading and literary website. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the content currently provided on the site.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the website changes, particularly if new features, contact forms, analytics, subscriptions, or other services are added in the future.

Questions

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to Bradley Hobbs through the appropriate contact information associated with this website.
