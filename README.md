# The Weight of the Pulpit | A Joseph Bennett Story

A Christian teaching project examining the responsibility of preaching, pastoral leadership, spiritual authority, and the weight of handling God's Word faithfully.

## Live Website
https://brad-hobbswd.github.io/weight-pulpit/

## Project Overview
The Weight of the Pulpit: A Joseph Bennett Story by Bradley Hobbs. A story about ministry, friendship, leadership, exhaustion, grace, and discovering the difference between serving people and losing yourself.
