About the Author

Bradley Hobbs

A writer who believes that stories can make room for the questions people are often afraid to ask.

Bradley Hobbs is a writer whose work explores faith, identity, relationships, ministry, and the experiences people often carry quietly.

His writing is shaped by a belief that faith should make room for honesty. Questions do not always represent a lack of faith, and exhaustion does not always mean a person has lost their purpose. Sometimes people simply need the freedom to acknowledge what they are carrying.

Through his writing, Bradley creates space for subjects that are often difficult to discuss openly. His work explores the complicated places where faith, friendship, leadership, responsibility, and personal identity meet.

The Weight of the Pulpit continues that exploration through the story of Joseph Bennett, a man who loves the church and believes deeply in serving others but begins to recognize that caring for everyone around him has left little room to acknowledge what he is carrying himself.

Sometimes the strongest thing a person can do is admit that they are carrying more than they were ever meant to carry alone.

Bradley's writing does not attempt to offer easy answers to complicated experiences. Instead, he believes that stories can create a place where readers feel seen and understood while they begin asking their own questions.

The Weight of the Pulpit is part of that purpose. It is a story about the people who serve, the people who lead, the friendships that shape them, and the quiet weight that can develop when someone spends so much of their life caring for others that they forget they are also a person who needs care.
