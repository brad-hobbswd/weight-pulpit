A Joseph Bennett Story

The Weight We Carry in Silence

Joseph Bennett loved the church.

As an ordained teacher, ministry had given him purpose. He believed the church should be a place where people could come as they were, find grace, and discover that they did not have to prove they belonged before they were welcomed.

But ministry did not prevent exhaustion.

As friendships became complicated, conversations traveled farther than they should, and leaders continued carrying responsibilities that rarely ended when the service was over, Joseph found himself asking questions he had spent much of his life avoiding.

He cared about the people around him. He understood their struggles. He could see their exhaustion.

What he was beginning to question was whether he had ever learned to see his own.

What happens when the person who serves everyone else begins to ask who is carrying him?

The Weight of the Pulpit is a story about ministry, friendship, leadership, gossip, exhaustion, grace, boundaries, and the quiet realization that serving people does not always mean carrying everything they bring into your life.
